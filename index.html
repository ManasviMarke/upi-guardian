How It Works

Why combining two signals catches more fraud than either alone

📈

1. Learn normal patterns

We look at amount, timing and payee to see if a payment is unusual.

💬

2. Scan messages

Recent SMS or call text is checked for scam language like urgency and OTP requests.

🛡️

3. Fuse the signals

An unusual payment alone, or a scary message alone, may be harmless. Both together, close in time, is a strong red flag.

The fusion rule

If a message scored above 50% scam in the last 15 minutes, the transaction score is multiplied by 1.5 and a bonus is added. The final score is capped at 100.
